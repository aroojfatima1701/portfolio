Misk & Mirr

Misk is musk and mirr is myrrh, two of the oldest scents of Arabia.

I designed the logo as an M shaped like a perfume bottle with a cut-gem stopper. Oxblood on blush keeps it warm and feminine, and the arch from Gulf doorways frames the photography across the Shopify store.

APX Athletics

A gymwear brand that shouts the way lifters train: loud lime on black, all muscle and no noise.

I designed the full identity: a flexing bodybuilder mark with a dumbbell, the bold italic APXATHLETICS wordmark and the line “Gymwear & Essentials”. Volt lime on black works on everything from a drawstring bag and a shaker bottle to posters, wristbands and an Instagram grid.

Saatio

“Saat” means hour or time in Urdu and Turkish, close to the Arabic ساعة.

The four-petal symbol came as a sketch in the brief. I redrew it as clean vectors, kept the single coral petal, set the wordmark and turned the petal’s shape into the corner style for cards, chips and buttons across the product.

Regalia Aesthetics

A doctor-led clinic that should feel calm, expert and quietly luxurious.

The logo was supplied with the brief. I built the visual system around it: plum and soft sand instead of the usual clinic white, an editorial serif, gold used sparingly, and an Arabic typeface chosen to match the English one.
