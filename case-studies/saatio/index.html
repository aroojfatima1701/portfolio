Booking software that answers WhatsApp for you

Saatio is a concept booking and scheduling product for salons, clinics, spas and gyms in the UAE and the USA. I designed and built all of it: the marketing website, a client booking page, the business dashboard with an AI receptionist, and the admin panel Saatio’s own team would use to run the platform.

The brief

The owner is also the receptionist

In Dubai most small salons and clinics take bookings on WhatsApp. The owner or a stylist answers between clients, often hours later, and checks a paper diary or a shared phone to see what is free.

That costs them in three ways. Messages that arrive late at night go unanswered and the client books somewhere else. Quiet hours in the middle of the week stay empty. And no-shows leave a stylist idle with no one to call. The big booking platforms solve part of this, but they are built for the US or Europe, price in dollars and don’t speak Arabic or the Roman Urdu many staff and clients actually type. So I set four goals for Saatio.

Answer every messageAn AI receptionist on WhatsApp that books, moves and cancels from live availability, any time of day.
Fill the quiet hoursFind empty gaps in the week and offer them to clients who are due a visit.
Stop no-shows fairlyReminders for everyone, and a deposit only for the few clients who often miss.
Run as a real SaaSPlans in AED and USD, a free trial, and an admin panel for the Saatio team.
Brand

A name about time

“Saat” means hour or time in Urdu and Turkish, close to the Arabic ساعة, so it reads naturally to the people who will use it in the Gulf. The “io” gives it a product feel. We checked several names first; some were already taken.

The client designed the four-petal symbol as an image. I redrew it as clean vectors, kept its shape and the single coral petal exactly, and set the wordmark beside it. The petal shape then became the corner style for cards, swatches and chips across the product.

Marketing website

Sell it by showing it

A business owner decides in a minute or two. So instead of screenshots and promises, the website lets them use the product: every section links into the working demo.

Booking page

A link for Instagram bios

Every business gets its own booking page to share on Instagram, Google and WhatsApp. The sample salon is Noor Beauty Lounge in Dubai Marina.

  • Deposits only when neededMost clients book without paying anything. A client with two or more past no-shows is asked for a deposit, 25% by default.
  • Recognises regularsTyping a known phone number greets the client by name, so returning clients don’t fill in the same details again.
  • Lands in the calendarA booking made here appears in the business dashboard at once, marked as an online booking.
  • Local timeTimes are shown in the business’s own time zone, so a client planning from abroad sees the salon’s clock.
AI receptionist

Writes back the way clients write

Clients in Dubai message in English, Arabic or Roman Urdu, often mixed and full of typos. The receptionist understands all three, answers in the client’s language and books straight into the calendar.

Kal shaam 5 baje blow-dry ho sakta hai?
Saatio AISara kal 4:30 PM par aap ki blow-dry kar sakti hain. Kaunsa time theek rahega?
kitne ka hai?
Saatio AIBlow-dry AED 120 ki hai aur is mein 45 minute lagta hai. Upar wale times mein se koi book kar dun?
koi offer hai?

If 5 PM is taken, it offers the nearest free times instead of saying no. It remembers what the conversation is about, so “how much is it?” gets the price of that service, not the whole menu, and “yes” books the time it just offered.

Questions it can’t handle, like a complaint, go to the team with the chat attached.

  • Offers the owner controlsWhen a client asks about discounts, it lists the salon’s current offers, which the owner edits in Settings, one per line.
  • Moves and cancelsIt finds the client’s upcoming bookings, asks which one, and offers new free times.
  • New clientsSomeone not yet in the system is asked their name before the booking is made, and is added as a client.

How the demo works: in this concept the receptionist runs offline in the browser, using a language engine I wrote that matches intents, fuzzy-matches typos and keeps track of the conversation. In the real product, the same actions would be driven by an AI model connected to the WhatsApp Business API and the business’s calendar.

Business dashboard

The whole salon on one screen

What the owner opens in the morning: today’s bookings, revenue, how many bookings came online or through the AI, and the no-show rate.

Platform admin

For the team running Saatio

A SaaS needs a back office too. This is where the Saatio team sees every business on the platform, monthly recurring revenue, trials and plans.

Mobile

Run it from the salon floor

Owners rarely sit at a desk. Every part of Saatio works on a phone, from the booking page to the AI chat.

Build

One product, fully connected

Built with React and TypeScript, with the data layer specified as a Laravel API.
