Overview

The brief

Blinks is my own independent project: an online marketplace for Pakistan, similar in scope to the large platforms in the region, selling almost every kind of product from many independent sellers.

The challenge was not only the shop itself. In Pakistan, most buyers pay cash on delivery, sellers need a clear picture of what they will be paid, and the business needs control over every commercial rule without calling a developer each time.

The goal was a complete first launch: buyers, sellers and an admin team working in one system, with money, deliveries and returns handled correctly from day one.

Complete work process

From a new seller to a paid order

How buyers, sellers and the Blinks team work together, step by step.

  1. SellerApplies to sell

    Enters store details, ID card images and bank account. Sensitive details are encrypted.

  2. AdminReviews and approves the store

    Checks the documents. The seller's private panel opens only after approval.

  3. SellerLists products

    Adds photos, sizes, prices, stock and weight. Banned words are blocked automatically.

  4. AdminApproves each product

    Nothing appears in the shop until the Blinks team approves it.

  5. BuyerBrowses and fills the cart

    Searches, filters and adds items as a guest. No account is needed until checkout.

  6. BuyerChecks out

    Logs in or signs up, picks an address and pays cash on delivery or online. Prices and stock are checked again at this moment.

  7. SellerPrepares the parcel

    Each store receives only its own part of the order, then marks it ready for the courier.

  8. AdminBooks the courier

    Prints the label and adds the tracking number. The buyer is kept updated.

  9. BuyerReceives and pays

    Pays the courier at the door, then can leave a review or request a return.

  10. AdminMatches the courier's cash

    Records the payment from the courier against each delivered parcel.

  11. AdminPays the seller

    After the hold period, the seller's balance is paid and shown on their statement.

Abilities

Built for heavy traffic and big campaigns

Traffic

Saved page copies

Busy shop pages are kept in memory for a short time, so a crowd does not hit the database on every visit. Checkout always reads fresh prices and stock.

Traffic

QR codes open a tiny page with no database work, built for thousands of scans at the same moment.

Traffic

Room to grow

The launch plan runs several copies of the site behind Cloudflare, with servers added on campaign days.

Orders

No overselling

When two buyers race for the last item, only one order goes through. Tested automatically.

Marketing

Campaign tracking

Every influencer or event gets its own link and QR code, showing visits, signups, orders and voucher cost.

Marketing

Single-use vouchers

Up to 20,000 one-time codes at once for printed cards, so a shared code gives no extra discount.

Safety

Fake order limits

New buyers have a cash on delivery limit until their first order arrives. Admin can change it.

Safety

Account protection

Wrong-password limits per account, instant blocking, and password reset links that work only once.

Operations

Background work

Emails, courier tracking and payment checks run separately, so buyers never wait for them.

Key decisions

Rules the business can change itself

Editable commission

A default rate that admin can change per category, saved on each order so past orders never change.

One parcel per store

Each seller ships separately, with delivery fees by weight. This matches how couriers in Pakistan charge and keeps each seller responsible for their own parcel.

Who pays for vouchers

Blinks, the seller or a shared split, chosen per voucher, so promotions can be agreed with sellers later.

Returns by fault

Damaged item, courier fault or changed mind each have a default payer that can be changed to match future contracts.

Money on hold

Sellers are paid only after the courier's cash is matched and a hold period ends, so returns never leave Blinks out of pocket.

Engineering details

Where the hard problems were

A few of the decisions in the code that keep money, stock and data correct.

Checkout in one transaction

Placing an order checks the total, empties the cart, reduces stock and creates the order, its parcels and items in a single database transaction. If any step fails, nothing is saved, so stock and money never drift apart.

A ledger that cannot be edited

Every sale, commission, refund and payout is a row in the seller ledger. A database trigger blocks edits and deletes; mistakes are fixed with an adjustment row, so a seller's statement always adds up.

One place decides the price

Normal prices, flash-sale prices and voucher discounts all go through one function at checkout, and each order item saves the price and commission rate it was sold at.

Data stays with its owner

Every seller query takes the store from the login session, never from the address bar or the form. The journey test tries to change another store's order and confirms it is refused.

Search that degrades gracefully

Meilisearch finds product IDs, but the database still decides what a buyer may see. If search is down, basic title search keeps the shop working.

Sensitive data encrypted

Seller ID card and bank numbers are encrypted at rest, and ID card photos are private files only the super admin can open.

Trust and safety

Protecting buyers, sellers and Blinks

  • Seller ID card and bank details stored encrypted
  • Nothing goes live until admin approves it
  • Seller money recorded in a ledger that cannot be edited
  • Limits on password guessing, and blocking of bad accounts
  • Single-use event vouchers that cannot be shared
  • A cash on delivery limit for brand-new buyers
Quality

A full order, tested automatically

An automated journey test runs the real system on its own database: buyer signup, store approval, ordering, delivery, cash matching, seller payout, returns and online payment.

It also tries the attacks that matter: one store changing another's order, two buyers racing for the last item, a faked payment message and a shared voucher code. All of them are stopped.

Lessons

What I learned

Make business rules editable

Commission, fees and return rules will change with contracts. Building them as settings saved a rebuild later.

Test the whole journey, not pieces

Hands-on checkpoints found problems that single-feature tests missed, like a negative seller balance after a return.

Marketing shapes the system

Big campaigns meant planning for fake accounts, voucher abuse and traffic spikes before launch, not after.

Legal comes before branding

The trademark check comes before the company, the domain and the logo, so the name is safe before money is spent on it.
