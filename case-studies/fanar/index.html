The brief

Look established on day one

A Dubai agency competes with brands that have spent years building trust. Buyers are often abroad, comparing agents on their phones and deciding in minutes who looks credible enough to call.

I set three goals. The brand had to feel premium and local at once. The website had to answer the questions Dubai buyers actually ask: Golden Visa eligibility, payment plans, service charges, permit numbers. And the agency needed its own system for leads, listings and viewings instead of renting one.

Research

What Dubai buyers expect

I reviewed the leading agencies in Dubai and the USA and noted the patterns buyers already trust. I kept those patterns and built Fanar's own identity on top of them.

  • Proof in the first screenA photographic hero with the search on it and key numbers directly below.
  • Quiet luxuryDeep navy, generous white space and serif headlines, the visual language of high-end property.
  • A clear pathPages that move from search, to reasons to trust the agency, to an easy way to make contact.
  • A signature detailOne italic word in every headline, so the brand is recognisable at a glance.
Brand

Fanar means lighthouse

The name gives the agency its story: a steady light that guides people home. I tested names for both markets and chose one that is easy to say in English and meaningful in Arabic.

Website

Designed around how Dubai buys property

Every page answers a question a buyer, tenant or seller in Dubai would ask before they pick up the phone.

  • Rentals the Dubai wayYearly rent with the number of cheques the landlord accepts.
  • Selling, step by stepForm A, the advertising permit, Form F, the developer NOC and the trustee transfer, explained in plain words.
  • International buyersPrices in AED, USD, GBP or EUR and areas in square feet or metres, remembered per visitor.
  • WhatsApp firstEvery adviser contact opens WhatsApp with a message that already names the property and permit.
Mobile

Most buyers arrive on a phone

Phones get lighter 720p hero videos, collapsible filters and a fixed WhatsApp and booking bar on property pages.

Admin panel

Designed to run the agency

Instead of paying monthly for a platform, the agency would own a custom panel: leads, listings, viewings, off-plan projects, team roles, website content and reports in one place.

The admin panel is a fully interactive front end with role-based access, designed to connect to a Laravel API.

  • Role-based accessAdmins see everything, managers see the whole team's work, advisers only their own leads, listings and viewings.
  • Listings with complianceA listing cannot be saved without its DLD permit number.
  • ReportsLead sources with win rates, the sales funnel and deals by adviser, so the owner can see which portals pay for themselves.
Build

Fast, findable and easy to use

Built with React and TypeScript in a Next.js structure, so the site loads quickly on any connection, search engines can read every listing, and everyone can use it.

  • PerformanceHero videos compressed from 50 MB to about 7 MB. Only the current clip loads; the next one loads while it plays.
  • RenderingPages render on the server first, so content shows before JavaScript loads and search engines can read every listing.
  • AccessibilityLabelled forms, keyboard-friendly drawers and galleries, visible focus states, and still images for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
