A perfume house for the Gulf, in two languages

Misk & Mirr is a concept oud and perfume brand for the UAE. I designed the logo and the brand, built a custom Shopify theme, and set up everything behind it: products with scent notes, a scent quiz, a full Arabic version that reads right to left, delivery rates, a discount code and the store’s policy pages.

The brief

You can’t smell a website

Perfume is one of the biggest online categories in the UAE, but it is also the hardest to sell on a screen. Shoppers want to know how a scent smells, how long it lasts and whether it suits the occasion before they spend AED 400 on a bottle.

Gulf shoppers also expect a store to speak Arabic properly, not as a translated afterthought, and a lot of perfume is bought as a gift for Eid, weddings and visits. So I set four goals for the store.

iDescribe the scentTop, heart and base notes, intensity, how long it lasts and how far it carries, on every product.
iiHelp people chooseA short quiz that matches a shopper to one perfume from three simple questions.
iiiArabic, done properlyEvery word translated, right-to-left layout, Arabic typefaces and a one-tap language switch.
ivReady to runReal delivery rates, a welcome discount, policy pages and fields the owner can edit without code.
Brand

Musk and myrrh

Misk is the Arabic word for musk, and mirr is myrrh. They are two of the oldest scents of Arabia, and the name says what the house makes in two short words that work in English and Arabic.

I designed the logo myself: an M drawn as a perfume bottle, with a cut gem for its stopper, beside a wordmark with a small ampersand. Oxblood on blush keeps it warm and feminine without looking like coffee or chocolate.

The storefront

A custom theme, block by block

I built the homepage from my own theme blocks: an arched hero, a band of key notes, the collection grid, mood tiles, the scent quiz, a brand story and a gifting band. Each block has settings, so the owner can change images, text and products in the Shopify editor.

Product page

Notes you can read

Every product has eight custom fields: a tagline, top, heart and base notes, scent family, intensity from 1 to 5, longevity and projection. A theme block turns them into a notes card beside the price.

The fields are proper Shopify metafield definitions, pinned to the product page in the admin. The owner fills in a new perfume like a form, and intensity only accepts a number from 1 to 5, so the dots can’t break.

Scent quiz

Three questions, one perfume

The quiz asks when you’ll wear it, which notes you love and how strong you like it. I wrote it as a small web component in plain JavaScript. The theme passes it each product’s tags, intensity, image and price as JSON, and the quiz scores every perfume and shows the best match with a link to buy.

Because it reads live product data, a new perfume joins the quiz as soon as it is tagged. Nothing in the code needs to change.

Arabic version

Right to left, not just translated

Arabic shoppers get the whole store in Arabic at /ar, and the layout mirrors itself: the logo moves to the right, the hero image swaps sides and every row reads right to left.

A switch in the header moves between ENالعربية and keeps you on the same page. On phones it shrinks to one button for the other language, so the header still fits at 390 pixels.

  • Every word in ArabicProducts, notes and taglines, collections, menus, the policy pages, delivery options at checkout and the search-engine titles, all through Shopify’s translation API.
  • Theme text in a locale fileAll my theme’s own words, from quiz questions to button labels, live in English and Arabic locale files, not in the templates.
  • Arabic typefacesNoto Naskh Arabic for headings and IBM Plex Sans Arabic for text load only on Arabic pages, and letter-spacing is turned off so Arabic letters stay joined.
  • Direction from ShopifyThe page direction comes from the shopper’s language setting in Shopify, so layout spacing uses start and end, not left and right.
Behind the store

Set up to sell

A store is more than its pages. I set up the admin side so the owner could start taking orders, and so every promise on the website matches what checkout actually does.

  • Delivery that matches the websiteThree rates for the UAE: standard AED 25, free on orders of AED 300 or more, and express next-day AED 45. The homepage promises free delivery over AED 300, so checkout gives exactly that.
  • Welcome discountCode WELCOME10 takes 10% off a first order, once per customer, and can be combined with free delivery.
  • Policy pagesShipping and delivery, returns and refunds, terms of service and a FAQ, in English and Arabic, linked in the footer. Returns follow perfume rules: unopened bottles within 14 days.
  • CatalogueSix products with sizes, prices in dirhams, SKUs and tracked stock, grouped into five collections.
  • Navigation and searchA main menu by scent family, a footer of policy pages, and a search title, description and image alt text for every product.
