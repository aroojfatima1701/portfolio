How selling works

Five steps to sold

  1. 01

    Free valuation

    We price your home from recent sales in your building, not from asking prices on the portals.

  2. 02

    Form A and permit

    We sign the RERA listing agreement (Form A) with you and obtain the DLD advertising permit.

  3. 03

    Photography and launch

    Professional photos, video and a floor plan, then your home goes live to our buyer list and the main portals.

  4. 04

    Viewings and offers

    We qualify every buyer before a viewing and negotiate on your behalf.

  5. 05

    Transfer

    We manage the MOU (Form F), the bank, the NOC from the developer and the transfer at the DLD trustee office.
