Off-plan payment plans: 60/40 vs post-handover

By Omar Siddiqui, Off-plan Specialist

Payment plans are what make off-plan attractive: you pay in stages instead of all at once. But the split matters more than most buyers realise.

How a 60/40 plan works

You pay 60% in instalments while the building rises and 40% when you receive the keys. Your money is held in an escrow account regulated by the Dubai Land Department until each stage is reached.

Post-handover plans

Some developers let you keep paying for two to five years after handover. Rent from the unit can cover part of those payments, but the overall price is usually higher.

What to check

Look at the developer's record of delivering on time, the escrow registration and the service charge estimate. A slightly lower price is not worth a two-year delay.
