About me

Designer’s eye, developer’s hands.

I’m Arooj Fatima, a full-stack developer and designer based in Lahore, Pakistan. I like owning the whole journey of a product: the name and logo, every screen, the code behind it and the admin tools the business uses every day.

I started in graphic design, creating logos, brand identities and product advertising. I wanted my designs to work, not only look good, so I moved into web development. My first websites were online stores and business sites, each with its own admin panel, like APX Athletics, HNT Essentials and AL-RAZA Travels & Tours.

In 2026 I took on much bigger work. I built Blinks, a multi-vendor marketplace for Pakistan, completely on my own: the database, checkout, payments, couriers, returns, the seller panel and the admin panel. Alongside it I designed and built products for the UAE and US markets: Saatio, booking software with an AI receptionist; Regalia, a bilingual clinic website; Fanar, a luxury real estate platform; Crumb & Crema, a café ordering site; and Misk & Mirr, a bilingual Shopify store.

What I care about: pages that are clear and fast, Arabic that reads correctly right to left, admin screens a non-technical owner can use, and the details nobody sees, like orders that can never lose money or stock.

I’m now looking for a full-time role with a team in the UAE or USA, remote or on-site with relocation, where design and engineering work side by side.

Honest note: Blinks is my own independent product. The 2026 UAE and US projects are self-initiated concepts, built to the standard of client work, with sample data.
